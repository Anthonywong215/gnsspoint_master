WGS84→HK80 + mPD
WGS84 latitude, longitude + ellipsoidal height → HK80 northing, easting + height in mPD

HK80 means the HK1980 Grid. Output height is metres above Hong Kong Principal Datum (mPD). Confirm your source uses WGS84 and ellipsoidal height; the numbers alone cannot identify the datum.

    hkN HK80 northing · mhkE HK80 easting · mhkpd HK height · mPD

    Standard exports use Elevation; Topcon exports use 84H for WGS84 ellipsoidal height, with B as latitude and L as longitude. Topcon’s Elevation and E/N/Z, and a standard export’s separate Height, are ignored. Do not supply existing mPD or orthometric height as ellipsoidal height.
